## Facts stated (by Maria Fernanda Lemos, unless noted)

**Intake channels** — Slack (canal dedicado), DM direta, boca a boca. A
form posted in the Slack channel notifies the team and feeds an Airtable
base. She pushes people to always open a task themselves, but opens it
herself when they don't.

**Demand types**: suporte pontual (permissões, automações, formulários em
base); apoio na criação de agentes/soluções mais complexas; discovery de
produto em menor escala.

**Registration**: every request lands in Airtable — *"a fonte de verdade
do time."* This is the demand-intake log, distinct from any record of
individual skill progression (see Open questions above).

**Support method**: first, understand context and complexity. She asks the
person to take the first step alone; when the ask is underspecified or
needs more context, she sits with them directly (example: 30 min with
Ana). For complex demands, she breaks the work into smaller steps and
guides step by step — worked example: Jorge's idea for an agent that
searches the Matriz and pulls a briefing from the internet; she structured
the build sequence, and he ended up running the whole thing alone with
just an Anthropic key.

**Escalation to the technical layer**: when a request exceeds her own
understanding, she redirects to the right person. Worked example: Osmar
built a Vercel app for external-event registration (the same use case
recorded in [[Agent Flow]]'s *"use case that arrived from the business"*
section); msilva redirected him to Arthur Tavares because it touched the
data matrix. The Airtable task gets reassigned from the support person to
the technical owner — she flags this reassignment step as something that
could be better structured.

**Pattern → institutional project**: she watches for the same request
recurring across different people. Two worked examples: (1) mapping 36
contacts revealed heavy, independent use of Google Earth Studio and other
external tools → proposal for a unified environment; (2) many people
separately searching for content on Instagram/Twitter → became an
in-progress project (with the systems team) for easier social-media
access. When she spots an institutional-scale pattern, she brings it to
Gabi for evaluation and routing.
