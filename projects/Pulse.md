# Pulse

Front-end for Livemode's **esteira de negócios** (business pipeline). Not
msilva's project — recorded because he is expected to join its immersion week for
context on the company's legacy and business rules.

## What it is

- A front-end application covering the whole business pipeline.
- **Built by an external partner**, DOR, after an earlier vendor engagement
  failed and the project was redesigned.
- Classed as a **corporate project** on the "sensitive financial data" criterion
  — see [[2026-08-14 Papo de Projetos]].

## The immersion week

A week on site with the partner to talk to every area and close scope, acceptance
rules, and modules. Includes a technical session where Luís hands over the legacy
code inheritance to the partner's CTO.

**msilva's role is context, not delivery** — Gabrielle's rationale was that
understanding how the company's product actually works makes everything else
easier.

## Why it slipped (2026-08-17)

The blocker was **financial approval**, not the contract:

- Approval was not obtained the week of 2026-08-11. **Ribon was on leave**, and both
  **Zoca and Cristian wanted his sign-off alongside their own** — so it could not
  proceed without him. Earliest possible is the week of 2026-08-24.
- **DOR offered to come and run the workshop anyway**, without approval in hand. The
  team declined. Rationale: the project would be presented to three people
  (Cristian, Ribon, Zoca) who *"não [estão] há muito tempo na empresa"*, with little
  read on what would come back from them. Judged too risky.
- Instead the project goes through **a second approval round at a higher seniority
  level**, and will be re-presented the week of 2026-08-24. DOR has also offered to
  come and present their own view of the project.

**Consequences, stated openly in the meeting and accepted jointly:**

- The project **cannot start until Gabrielle returns**, since she is on leave from
  the week of 2026-08-24 — the presenter *"já dei um disclaimer"* that an important
  window would be lost.
- **Delay of at least one month**, which **loses the Copinha** — the intended
  production test. Framed carefully as opportunities forgone rather than risks
  materializing.

**The escalation is considered a net positive**, not damage control. The argument:
the four people under **Sérgio** should be bought in before the project reaches
Sérgio, and *"quanto mais alto nível a gente colocar o projeto, mais todo mundo vai
estar comprado em fazer ele acontecer"* — because the project's central challenge is
**engagement across the company**, not delivery.
