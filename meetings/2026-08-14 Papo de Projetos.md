## What's relevant

**Criteria for a "corporate project".** A project is corporate when it is
essential to operations, involves sensitive financial data, or requires
validating complex flows across multiple areas. A governance document is being
written to help managers make that call.

This **resolves the open "Orca" question** carried on [[Agent Flow]] and
[[2026-08-10 Onboarding Técnico - Matheus]]: **Orca is a real system at Livemode,
cited as the example of a project essential to operations.** That makes it a
credible target for the first monitoring agent, alongside [[LiveScript]] — it was
never a transcription artifact.

[[Pulse]] was cited as the sensitive-financial-data example.

**Habilitadores.** Most of the area works as enablers, helping other departments
build their own products. Whether they offer consulting, project management, or
direct development depends on the project's complexity and demand. This confirms
the "enablers" front described in [[Agent Flow]] from an independent source.

**A documentation Hub exists.** A centralized page covering Livemode, the area's
structure, current projects, and reference documents — functioning as an intranet
substitute, edited via GitHub. Currently being integrated with Airtable and
**known to contain inaccuracies**.
