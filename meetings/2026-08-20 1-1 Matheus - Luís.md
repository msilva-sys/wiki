## Facts stated

- Luís: msilva is already doing the agent-inventory work informally, even
  though it hasn't been done "officially" yet.
- msilva's own heuristic, arrived at while testing each of the 14 agents:
  **agir versus informar** (act vs. inform). An agent *acts* — changes state,
  writes code, produces a report, alters material against a standard — or
  *reasons* over its input toward a judgment. **Watch/A5** is the clean
  positive case: it takes metrics and inputs and reasons over them to flag
  something as a **bad pattern**, not just relaying numbers.
- Something that only **informs** — plain input in, data out, no
  orchestration, no reasoning, "just one LLM call" — is not a standalone
  agent. It's a **skill or capability living inside another agent**, and as
  a fifteenth entity it effectively "dies" — it doesn't need to exist on its
  own.
- **Teacher** was floated as the candidate that might fail this test — purely
  informational rather than acting or reasoning. Not resolved.
- Luís's framing of what these entities are: he treats them like coworkers —
  *"para mim são pessoas, eu trato esses caras [...] como companheiras de
  trabalho, eu tô dando capacidade para ele"* — a personal philosophy he says
  he uses in his own workshops.
- Luís's core feedback: msilva has been more oriented toward **how** to build
  (implementation, memory design, "cloud code") than toward **what** to
  build — a discovery/product problem, which he says is the team's actual
  job right now. *"Você ainda não sabe o que que você quer."*
- The memory-structure question msilva had started on (which entity owns
  which memory; whether it's a Linear-native responsibility, a standalone
  app monitoring webhooks, or Claude Code itself) is explicitly premature per
  Luís — settle which agents exist first, then bring build options (A/B/C)
  once Gabrielle and Carol's appetite for pace is understood.
- Luís's validation method for the inventory: do the work and present it
  plainly. A component that fails the test should be self-evidently
  indefensible — *"isso aqui, galera, não faz sentido nenhum [...] não tem
  como contra-argumentar"* — while one that survives will show real
  capabilities and consultations that justify it.
- The call ends with Luís noticing/asking about the Obsidian vault itself
